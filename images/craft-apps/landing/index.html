03 / Notes

Where your work lives

Each app runs on your device. Open reads a file you pick, Save and Export download one. Preferences stay in this browser. LightCraft keeps its photo library in this browser's own storage, so clearing this site's data removes it.

What your browser needs

A current browser with WebGPU or WebGL2. The apps fall back from WebGPU to WebGL2 on their own; add ?webgl to an app's address to force it. The first open of each app downloads it, from 2 to 70 MB.

Who makes them

These are open-source projects by the ArtCraft team and community (getartcraft.com), licensed MIT or Apache-2.0. This page serves their official web builds unmodified; each app's licence and notices sit in its folder. Hola is not affiliated with them.
